categories: ["Postres i dolços"]
lang: "en"
date: 2021-10-22
lastmod: 2021-10-22
---

- 2 packs of cream cheese
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla
- ⅔ cup sugar
- ⅔ sour cream (optional)

Use room temp ingredients. Mix all but eggs.
Add eggs last, but don't overmix!
Put in crust.
Bake for 1 hr @ 350° F.
Let cool, serve chilled.
